My research centers on how teachers and learners grow through active, technology-supported education. Below are the main themes that connect my publications.

### Flipped and blended teacher education
I study flipped professional development and blended learning for pre-service and in-service English teachers, with attention to engagement, attitude, achievement, professionalism, and organizational commitment.

### Assessment, motivation, and learner outcomes
This work examines online dynamic assessment, high-stakes testing contexts, achievement goals, attainment emotions, educational accountability, and creativity among EFL learners.

### Intercultural awareness and ESP pedagogy
I also work on cultural identity, cultural intelligence, critical intercultural awareness, and classroom approaches such as concept mapping for ESP reading comprehension.

### Meta-research on flipped classrooms
Together with colleagues, I synthesize evidence on flipped classroom practice to inform teacher education at Farhangian University and beyond.
