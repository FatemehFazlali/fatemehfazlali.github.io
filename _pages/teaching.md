I teach and design courses in English language teaching and teacher education at Farhangian University, with an emphasis on active, technology-supported learning.

### Areas of teaching interest
- Classroom management for novice EFL teachers
- Flipped and blended learning in language education
- Professional development for pre-service and in-service teachers
- Reading comprehension and ESP pedagogy
- Research methods in applied linguistics / TEFL

### Professional development
I have designed and delivered flipped professional development courses (including classroom management) for novice English teachers in language institutes and schools in Tehran, using learning management systems such as Farhangyada to support engagement and achievement outside the traditional lecture format.
