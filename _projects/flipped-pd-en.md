Flipped professional development reverses the usual lecture-and-homework pattern so that teachers engage with content before class and use face-to-face time for active practice.

This line of research examines how flipped PD courses (for example, classroom management) affect novice English teachers’ engagement, attitudes, and achievement compared with traditional lecture-based training.
